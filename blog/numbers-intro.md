B1–B5 were rerun on Oct 26 on top of main. The earlier box run (Solar `1343cf25`/`4d86a23b`, solc 0.8.37) is superseded; its raw files are in git history at `cb0cc9508`.

**Box:** boxctl `m4-metal-large` in FRA, AMD EPYC 9254 (24 cores, 48 threads), 377 GiB RAM. Five boxes of this plan split the work, and each ran one benchmark at a time and nothing else: B1; B2 then evm2; B4; B3 then B5 then a repeat of evm2; safe Solady on main. Load average was under 3 at the start of each run, except B3 and safe Solady, which started at one-minute loads of 8.7 and 6.3 right after their box's build had finished, and the first evm2 run, which started at 6.0 right after B2. Toolchain: rustc 1.99.1 stable, Foundry/Anvil 1.8.6, uv 0.13.5. Solar was built with `cargo build --locked --profile bench -p solar-compiler --bin solar` at `fe4b93c2`, which is main `0303bb7d` plus this branch's blog files and the harness override below; the compiler code is main's. oksolc 0.2.2 was built from tag `v0.2.2` with Zig 0.17.0 (`ReleaseFast`, native CPU). solc 0.8.38 (`solc-static-linux`), solx 0.1.8 and the Slang preview (`slang-linux-amd64-gnu-debug-fast` from release `5e6ec5e1`, still the newest one) are the official binaries, checked against their published sha256. No compiler had a newer release than these on Oct 26.

**Flags:** `--mode runtime compile-time --suite all --compile-repeats 5 --repeat-long-compiles --gas --gas-profile hot --start-anvil --allow-failures`, so every compile, including slow ones, has five samples and tables report the median. B3 and B5 skip `--gas`.

**Shared config (B1–B3):** `--evm-version cancun` plus `BENCH_VIA_IR=1 BENCH_RELAX_PRAGMAS=1` (see `with_common_settings` in `benches/runtime/benchmark.py`). This is the one setting every compiler accepts:

- solx only parses Cancun or later targets.
- oksolc only supports via-IR.
- solx 0.1.8's frontend is solc 0.8.34, so the relaxed pragma (`>=0.8.0`) lets it build Tempo Zones.

The forced via-IR also changes solc's results:

- It makes solc much slower on projects that normally use the legacy pipeline, such as Solady (76 s instead of 17 s).
- It makes solc's gas worse where legacy was better. OpenZeppelin P256 costs 709,111 gas with via-IR against 534,674 with legacy.

Use B4 for claims about solc on each project's own settings.

**Original config (B4, B5, CI):** each case keeps its archived settings: EVM target, optimizer runs and pipeline.

**Aggregates:** equal-weight geometric means of per-case Solar/reference ratios over the cases both compilers built. Gas is the harness's comparable receipt gas summed per workload, including intrinsic and calldata gas, and excluding the gas-dependent LibString brutalizer calls. "Speedup" is reference median time divided by Solar's median time. Solar runs with its default 8 threads unless marked `-j1`; solc is single-threaded, solx and oksolc use their defaults (oksolc `--parallel --jobs 8`).

## Executed opcodes and evm2 timings

**Executed opcodes** (`box/opcodes-per-case.json`, tables in `solar-blog-evidence/opcodes/tables.md`): every hot-profile runtime call traced through anvil 1.8.6-nightly `debug_traceTransaction` with `opcodes/tracer.js`, counting the 515 calls in 26 cases that succeed under all three compilers and that the runtime bench compares (Tempo Zones portal is out because solc legacy hits stack too deep). Execution gas excludes intrinsic gas; every call's attribution matches its receipt. Same Solar commit, solc 0.8.38.

| JUMP | 6,933 | 73,941 | 69,104 | 10.7× / 10.0× fewer |

At optimizer runs 1 (Solar -Osize), Solar executes 1,437,548 opcodes against 1,868,076 and 1,802,510 (−23.0% / −20.2%), 47,392 JUMPs against 74,473 and 69,116, and 6,078,724 execution gas against 7,705,131 and 7,504,852 (−21.1% / −19.0%). The previous run, on `67cd2fc9f`, measured 1,247,303 opcodes and 5,371,599 execution gas for Solar -Ogas; the solc numbers are unchanged.

**evm2** (`box/evm2-per-case.json`, `solar-blog-evidence/evm2/`): the same 515 calls replayed as deploy-and-call bundles (`evm2/bundle.py`) through `evm2 bundle` on the interpreter and the JIT. evm2 is the comb4 source (evm2 main `c4cfe1eb` plus #799, #796 and the local bundle harness), built with `--profile profiling --all-features -Ctarget-cpu=znver4`; the JIT is LLVM at -O2 with gas metering, Osaka. Each call runs 7 timed batches and takes the median; each total sums the per-call minimum over 5 rounds, pinned to one core. Cycles and instructions are user-mode perf counters; evm2 does not report wall time. A repeat on a second box matched the cycle totals within 0.6% and the instruction totals within 0.01%.

| Solar vs solc legacy | Interpreter cycles | Interpreter instructions | JIT cycles | JIT instructions |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| runs 200 (Solar -Ogas) | −24.5% | −25.0% | −14.6% | −11.8% |
| runs 1 (Solar -Osize) | −17.6% | −18.2% | −13.5% | −9.2% |

Against the cheaper of solc legacy and via-IR per case, Solar is −20.5% / −9.9% (interpreter / JIT cycles) at runs 200 and −12.7% / −8.6% at runs 1; in instructions −21.4% / −8.6% and −13.8% / −5.9%. The JIT runs more instructions than solc legacy on seeded-words (+8.0%), verified-words (+3.7%) and solady-encoding (+2.8%) at runs 200. On the dev box the original comb4 binary (native Zen 5 build) gives −21.9% / −8.6% and −14.1% / −6.0% instructions against the best solc, the same as its 67cd2fc9f measurement (−21.8% / −8.6% and −14.0% / −6.0%).

## Caveats

- The whole-project geomean against solc depends on the config: 83× with forced via-IR, 33× on the projects' own settings. Only Morpho Blue is over 100× on its own settings.
- Solar's 8 threads account for 4.7× of its whole-project speed (geomean of `-j1` over default time across the 10 projects, B3 vs B1; Morpho 6.0×).
- solc 0.8.38 compiles 11–14% faster than 0.8.37 with via-IR (Morpho 29.6 s against 33.3 s) and 2–5% faster on legacy projects, so Solar's speedups over solc grew less than its own times fell. forge-std and PRBMath now land just under 100× in the shared config.
- Solar's peak RSS on whole projects rose since the previous run (Seaport 1,014 MiB against 632, Solady 408 against 322 in the shared config).
- Safe Solady and `@custom:solar-*` / `solarSafety` need the solar NatSpec tags; PR #1999 was closed in favour of split PRs, of which #2101 (tags), #2099 and #2100 are still open. The safe Solady section keeps the PR #1999 numbers. On main alone, without the tags, the safe port is cheaper on 78.3% of upstream tests (geomean 0.872×) and 0.894× by sum in the differential matrix (`safe-solady-main-*`); main plus #2101 rejects the port's `solar-scratch` blocks in LibSort, LibZip and WebAuthn.
- Box timings are about 40% slower per compile than the dev box (lower clock), but the ratios between compilers match.
- The Slang preview moves fast: the Oct 14 build (`5e6ec5e1`) compiles whole projects 2.1× faster than the Oct 9 build (`62d94366`) (geomean over the 7 projects both built; Morpho 3.6 s → 1.2 s) with nearly the same bytecode. Solar's lead over it is 6.71× on this run.
- The external Foundry suite has not been rerun on main for this draft.
